## The call in one paragraph

At ₹1,185 the market prices Infosys as if it stops growing: a reverse DCF implies just 3.6% a year
rupee revenue growth for ten years, roughly flat in constant currency. I think that is too bleak.
Q2 FY27 large-deal wins were $5.2 bn (62% net new, including a $2 bn+ deal that ramps up in FY28),
AI revenue rose to 10.5% of the total, and free cash flow is a 7.4% yield. This is a non-consensus
call: the median broker target is ₹1,108.

The main debate is the discount rate: on the raw bond yield the stock is only a HOLD.
The report explains why I think that double-counts India's country risk.
